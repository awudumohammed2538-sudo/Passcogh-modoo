🧪 Practical + Theory Exam Centre

Every subject should have both theory preparation and practical preparation where applicable.

📝 Theory Exam Guide

1. Read the command word carefully. 2. Answer exactly what is asked. 3. Show workings where required. 4. Use correct units, labels and terminology. 5. Review answers before submitting.

🧪 Practical Exam Guide

1. Identify apparatus/materials. 2. Follow safety rules. 3. Record observations accurately. 4. Draw clear labelled diagrams/tables. 5. State conclusions and calculations clearly.

Meet the Creator

PASSCOGH-MODOO was created by Awudu Mohammed Modoo Ayariga to make quality learning, revision, examination preparation and useful digital skills more accessible to Ghanaian learners.

The platform is intended for JHS, SHS/WASSCE, university and college learners, with a strong focus on understandable explanations, diagrams, practice and practical preparation.
